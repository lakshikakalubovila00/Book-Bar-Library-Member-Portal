Book Bar Library

The Book Bar Library, located in Padukka, is a vibrant community space dedicated to inspiring a lifelong love of reading and fostering a culture of learning. Designed to be calm, welcoming, and reader-friendly, the library provides an ideal environment for individuals of all ages to explore, discover, and grow through books. With a thriving membership of over 2,000 active readers, The Book Bar Library has become a trusted hub for knowledge and relaxation. Our extensive collection features more than 10,000 carefully curated books across a wide range of genres, including Fantasy, Mystery, Romance, Historical Fiction, Non-Fiction, and Children’s Literature. Whether you are a young reader beginning your journey, a student seeking knowledge, or an avid book lover searching for your next great read, The Book Bar Library offers something for everyone. Our mission is to create a space where imagination flourishes, learning never stops, and every visitor feels at home.
