Book Bar Library - Policies

Welcome to Book Bar Library, a modern library designed to provide a peaceful and knowledge-rich environment for readers of all ages. Our library combines traditional library services with modern digital facilities to create an enjoyable reading experience for members.

Membership Registration

The library offers several membership types for children and adults. Prospective members must complete the member registration form to obtain a library membership card. Registration can be completed at the library premises. Membership becomes active after successful registration and payment confirmation. Every registered member will receive a unique member number and membership card. All borrowers must have a valid membership account to borrow library materials.

Membership fees, Renewal fees, Reservation fees and Delay Fine fees may change in future according to library policies.

Guarantor Policy

Applicants under 16 years of age must register with a guardian. Applicants over 16 years of age must register with a guarantor. The guardian or guarantor must provide NIC number & Contact information.

Member Account & Online Access

Once membership is activated, members can access the online library system using their Member number & Registered password. Through the member portal, members can:

  • Search books
  • View borrowed books
  • View reservations
  • Check due dates
  • Reserve books
  • Renew Books
  • View fine charges
  • Receive notifications and reminders

Overdue, Lost & Damaged Books

Late Returns :
The library encourages timely returns to ensure fair access for all members. Members will receive reminders before and after the due date.

Overdue Charges :
If a borrowed item remains overdue for more than 30 days, the member may be temporarily suspended.

Lost Books :
If a book is lost, the member must pay the replacement value determined by the library.

Damaged Books :
Members are responsible for maintaining borrowed materials in good condition. If a book is severely damaged repair or replacement charges may apply according to the damage type and percentage.

Library Rules & Regulations

To maintain a peaceful reading environment, all visitors are expected to follow these rules:

  • Maintain silence inside the library
  • No smoking within the premises
  • No food or beverages inside reading areas
  • Handle books and library property carefully
  • Do not remove library materials without authorization
  • Respect library staff and other visitors

The library is not responsible for loss of personal belongings.
Any member violating library policies may face temporary suspension or permanent membership cancellation.
